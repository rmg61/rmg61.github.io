How are parts identified?

PartLab first finds existing bodies and disconnected mesh pieces. If it finds one connected piece, it looks for the neck and gaps between arms, legs, and torso in a roughly upright figurine.

Suggested regions are a preview. Exports use the assembled model. Mesh formats without units are treated as millimeters.
