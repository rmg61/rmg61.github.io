How are parts identified?

STEP bodies and source objects are preserved. Each mesh is split at disconnected triangle shells using shared edges, with a tiny coordinate tolerance. Colors identify geometric pieces, not semantic or mechanical roles.

A fused, connected model may remain one part. This prototype does not invent cuts or recognize items such as screws. OBJ materials/textures and 3MF print settings are not preserved. Mesh formats without units are treated as millimeters.
