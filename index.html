7InchDesktopDisplay

7InchDesktopDisplay is a personal, non-commercial desk display: a small 7-inch screen on a home desk that shows the time, weather, traffic, upcoming calendar appointments, recent email and home controls.

It is used by its owner only. It connects to the owner's own Google account to read calendar events and email, and to move unwanted email to Trash or mark it read when the owner asks it to on the display. It never sends email and does not share any data with anyone.
